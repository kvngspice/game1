  • Chain combos: land light, then medium, then heavy. Heavy knocks your rival down.
  • Stamina pays for attacks, dodges and guarding. Run dry while guarding and your guard breaks.
  • The Orí meter fills as you fight. When it glows, press Orí to enter your Divine State for nine seconds.
  • Dodge makes you untouchable for a moment. Hold toward your rival to dash through them.
  • Player 2 also works on the number pad (1 2 3, 4 special, 5 Orí, 0 dodge). Gamepads work too. Press Esc to pause, M to mute.
